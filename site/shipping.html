Where we deliver

We deliver across India through our courier partners. Some remote PIN codes may take longer or may not be serviceable; we'll tell you at checkout or contact you if that happens.

Processing time

Orders are packed and handed to the courier within 1 to 2 working days of being placed (or of payment being confirmed for UPI and card orders). Orders placed on Sundays or public holidays are processed the next working day.

Delivery time and charges

  • Standard: 3 to 6 working days after dispatch. Free on orders over ₹2,999; ₹99 below that.
  • Express: 1 to 2 working days after dispatch, in metro cities. ₹199.
  • Cash on delivery: available across serviceable PIN codes, with a ₹49 handling fee.

Tracking

When your order ships, you'll get a tracking number by email and WhatsApp. You can also check it any time on the Track your order page.

Delays and failed deliveries

Courier delays can happen during sales, festivals or bad weather. If a delivery fails because the address was wrong or nobody was available after repeated attempts, the parcel returns to us; we'll contact you to re-ship it (re-shipping charges may apply).

Damaged parcels

If your parcel arrives damaged or opened, please take photos and contact us within 48 hours of delivery.
