7-day returns and exchanges

You can return or exchange a piece within 7 days of delivery if it is unworn, unwashed and has its original tags and packaging.

How to start a return or exchange

  • Message us on WhatsApp [WhatsApp number] or email [support email] with your order number and the piece you want to return or exchange.
  • We'll arrange a pickup where available, or share the address to send it back.
  • For a size exchange, tell us the new size; we'll ship it once the original piece reaches us and passes a quality check.

Refunds

  • Once the returned piece passes our quality check, we refund within 5 to 7 working days.
  • UPI and card payments are refunded to the original payment method.
  • Cash on delivery orders are refunded by UPI or bank transfer to details you share with us.
  • Delivery and cash on delivery fees are [refundable / not refundable].

Damaged, defective or wrong items

If something arrives damaged, defective or isn't what you ordered, contact us within 48 hours of delivery with photos. We'll replace it or refund you in full, including delivery charges.

Cancellations

You can cancel an order before it ships by contacting us. Paid orders that are cancelled are refunded in full to the original payment method within 5 to 7 working days.
