This policy explains what personal information [Registered business name] ("we") collects through this website, why, and the choices you have. It is written to follow India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

What we collect

  • Order details: name, mobile number, email, delivery address and what you bought.
  • Payment: payments are handled by Razorpay. We never see or store your full card or UPI details; Razorpay processes them under its own policy.
  • Website data: your bag and discount code are saved in your own browser so they're still there when you return.
  • Messages: anything you send us on WhatsApp or email.

Why we use it

  • To process, deliver and track your order, and to handle returns and refunds.
  • To send order updates by email, SMS and WhatsApp.
  • To answer your questions.
  • To send offers, only if you've agreed to it. You can opt out any time.

Who we share it with

Only with the services needed to complete your order: our courier partners (name, phone, address), Razorpay (payment) and Google Workspace, where our order records are stored. We don't sell your personal data.

How long we keep it

We keep order records for as long as the law requires for accounts and tax purposes, and delete other personal data when it's no longer needed.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your personal data, or withdraw consent for marketing. Write to our grievance officer: [name], [email]. We'll respond within the time set by law.

Children

This shop is meant for adults. If you are under 18, please order with a parent or guardian's consent.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
