# Brand name shortlist: Vinaay Singh clothing brand

Date: 8 October 2026
Brief: a semi-luxury clothing brand for Gen Z, sold online. Vinaay's own idea: "The Unoriginal".

## What the research showed

- **Indian Gen Z semi-luxury names follow three patterns:**
  - ironic, self-aware phrases (Almost Gods, Fear of God)
  - respelled or blended words (Bluorng, Huemn, Corteiz)
  - short, punchy coined words or numbers (Six5Six, Toffle, Scuffers)
  Sources: Instagram "Indian streetwear brands" roundup (Jan 2026), Highsnobiety "25 streetwear brands to know in 2026".
- **"Everything looks the same" is a live complaint in Indian streetwear right now.** Brand reels and LinkedIn posts in 2026 open with lines like "Same fits. Same prints. Same energy." A brand that calls itself "unoriginal" and owns that joke speaks straight to it.
- This was a quick web search only. It is NOT a trademark, domain or Instagram handle check.

## Checks done so far

| Name | What I found |
|---|---|
| The Unoriginal / Unoriginal | Small brands abroad: "Unoriginal" (NYC, about 900 Instagram followers), a "Unoriginal" streetwear stall in New York state, "The Unoriginal Original" (Australia, about 50 followers). Nothing found in India. It's a common English word, so it's harder to own in search and to trademark. |
| UNORGNL | @unorgnl on Instagram is taken by a photographer. A brand would need a variant such as @unorgnl.in or @unorgnl.studio. |
| Reprise | **Taken in India**: reprise.co.in sells premium oversized tees and polos. Dropped. |
| Same Same | Used as a slogan ("Same same but different") by small Indian clothing stores. It's weak as a brand name. |

## Shortlist

**Built on Vinaay's idea**
1. **The Unoriginal**: an ironic, confident name in the same spirit as Almost Gods. It has a ready story: "everything's a copy, so we made the best one."
2. **UNORGNL**: the same idea, with the vowels dropped like Bluorng. It looks good on a tag or a chest print.
3. **(Un)Original**: a styled version that reads both ways, unoriginal and original.

**Same spirit, new words**
4. **Ditto**: means "the same again". Short and easy to brand.
5. **Déjà**: from déjà vu, "already seen". It sounds premium.
6. **Second Draft**: suggests reworked, refined and better the second time.
7. **Rerun**: a repeat, done better.
8. **Off Script**: ironic and rule-breaking, for Gen Z.

## Recommendation

- **First choice: The Unoriginal.** It's Vinaay's own idea, it has the strongest story, and it speaks to the "everything looks the same" mood. Use **UNORGNL** as the short logo or tag form.
- **Backups: Ditto or Second Draft.**

## Before finalising

Check these for the final 2 or 3 names:
1. Trademark: IP India public search, class 25 (clothing)
2. Domain: .com and .in
3. Instagram handle
4. Say it out loud to 3 or 4 people in the target age group, and check that it's easy to spell after hearing it once.

## Round 2 (10 October 2026): names that fit "Nothing is original. Inspired by you."

Ditto, Second Draft and Rerun are now product names on the site, so they're out as brand names.

| Name | Idea | Quick web check (not a trademark search) |
|---|---|---|
| **Copy That** | "Copy" (nothing is original) + "copy that" (got it, understood). Ironic, spoken Gen Z. | No clothing brand found; only used as a phrase. Promising. |
| **The Unoriginal / UNORGNL** | Vinaay's idea, current name on the prototype. | See round 1: no Indian brand found; @unorgnl taken on Instagram. |
| **Inspo** | Gen Z word for inspiration; ties to "inspired by you". | Not checked yet. Likely used by small stores; check. |
| **Trace** | Tracing over an original, made your own. Short, premium. | Not checked yet. |
| **Lookalike** | Playful copy joke, easy to say. | Not checked yet. |
| Borrowed | Everything is borrowed, from you. | "The Borrowed" exists (South Asian fashion resale, US). Risky. |
| Yours Truly | Inspired by you. | Taken (US streetwear label; Indian apparel company). Dropped. |
| Mirrorwork | Reflection + Indian mirror embroidery. | Search dominated by ethnic mirror-work wear. Dropped. |

Recommendation: keep **The Unoriginal (UNORGNL)** as first choice; **Copy That** as the strongest new alternative. Run trademark (IP India, class 25), domain and Instagram checks on both before deciding.

## Round 4 (10 October 2026): semi-luxury, unique (meaning "copy / reflection" underneath)

| Name | Meaning | Quick web check (not a trademark search) |
|---|---|---|
| **Simile** | A comparison: "like" something else. Nothing is original, everything is like something. Soft, literary, premium. | No clothing label found (one brand has a "Simile Shirt" product). **Promising.** |
| **Tracé** | French for "traced": drawn over an original, made your own. | Only used as a collection name; no label found. **Promising** (common French word, check trademark). |
| Calque | French for tracing paper / a word-for-word copy. Perfect meaning. | A small Chicago apparel label "Calque" exists. Risky. |
| Vellum | Tracing paper, fine parchment. | Taken (Vellum, Australian clothing label since 2013). |
| Reflet | French for "reflection". | Taken (Le Reflet clothing line). |
| Mimesis | Art theory word for imitation. | Taken (Mimesis fashion label). |
| Prati | Sanskrit "copy/again" (pratilipi). | Taken (PRATI cashmere; Indian label Prati). |
| Anuk | From anukaran, "imitation". | Taken (ANUK, Sri Lankan label). |
| Seconde | French "second". | Used (Seconde vintage, Montreal; Seconde Main). |
| Nakal | Hindi "copy". | Meme phrase ("Nakal ke liye bhi akal chahiye"); too jokey for semi-luxury. |

Tip: a "Maison" or "Studio" prefix lifts a simple word into semi-luxury (e.g. Maison Simile, Studio Tracé) and is easier to trademark than the bare word.

Current shortlist: **The Unoriginal**, **The Uninvented**, **Copy That** (ironic) / **Simile**, **Tracé** (semi-luxury).
